# Support the project

Phase Wake is free, without ads, without a subscription and without an account. If you would like to support
development voluntarily, you can send USDT to the addresses above. A donation unlocks nothing. Please check the
network before sending – blockchain transactions cannot be reversed and are publicly visible.

The addresses are listed **only on this page**, not in the apps and not in the store listings: Apple and Google require
their own payment systems for payments to developers inside apps.

Please report bugs and wishes as an issue on GitHub.
